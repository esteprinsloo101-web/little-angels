# Setup — Little Angels Grade R Micro-Pack (R99)

1. **Read** `DISCLAIMER.md` (practice/enrichment only; parent/guardian supervised).
2. **Print** worksheets from `worksheets/` on A4. Open `.html` in a browser → Print → Save as PDF if you like.
3. **Keep** `08_counting_ANSWERS.md` for the grown-up (answer key).
4. **Cut** word cards on the lines (optional: cardboard / laminate).
5. **Pick** one page per day — Grade R / ages ~4–6 pace. Don’t force a full set.
6. **Run** the 10-minute routine in `parent-guide/10_Minute_Daily_Routine.md`.
7. **Optional:** Free web practice games (ages 4–9 picker) — stars stay on-device only.

POPIA: do not upload kids’ names, photos, or school details into shared folders. Household use.
