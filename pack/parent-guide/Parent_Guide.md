# Parent Guide — Little Angels (Grade R micro-pack)

## Tone

Calm, short, kind. Practice is enrichment — not homework police.

## Scope

This pack is **Grade R / ages ~4–6** friendly practice: letters A–F, numbers 1–10, big shapes, simple word cards. It is **not** a full Foundation Phase curriculum and does **not** claim CAPS alignment.

## Answer key

See `worksheets/08_counting_ANSWERS.md` for counting and letter-match answers. Use it to support your child — not as a formal test score.

## Languages

Simple English, isiXhosa, and Afrikaans word cards are for **exposure**, not full language instruction. Use languages your family already speaks.

## Supervision

All activities are **parent/guardian-supervised**. Sit nearby. Celebrate tries.

## When to pause

Tired, hungry, upset, or bored → stop. Talk to your child’s teacher about school concerns. This pack does **not** diagnose learning difficulties.

## Privacy (POPIA)

Keep child data minimal. Free web app stars stay on-device. Don’t create under-18 accounts for this product.

## Legal reminder

Practice and enrichment worksheets only. Not CAPS-certified, not a school curriculum, and not a substitute for a registered teacher, school, or official DBE materials. Results vary; no exam or grade guarantees.
