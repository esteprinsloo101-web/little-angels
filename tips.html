Keep it short and warm

Ten calm minutes beat one stressed hour. Stop while it still feels fun.

  • Pick one module per day (letters or numbers — not both if energy is low).
  • Sit side-by-side. Celebrate tries, not only correct answers.
  • Use the age band picker so games stay in a gentle challenge zone.

Sample 10-minute routine

  • Minutes 1–2: Settle — water, bathroom, clear the table.
  • Minutes 3–7: One practice game (stars count as wins).
  • Minutes 8–10: One printable OR read 3 word cards together.

Languages at home

Simple English, isiXhosa, and Afrikaans word cards are for exposure and confidence — not full language teaching. Mix with the languages your family already speaks.

Printables tip

  • Print worksheets on A4; laminate word cards if you can reuse them.
  • Pencil grip and posture matter more than finishing every page.
  • Skip or simplify any page that frustrates your child.

When to pause

If your child is tired, hungry, or upset — stop. Practice should feel safe. Talk to their teacher if you have concerns about learning or development.
